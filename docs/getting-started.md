# Getting started

Tilt Shift Camera makes Farming Simulator 25 look like a model railway layout. It adds a
tilt-shift effect to the third-person views: a sharp strip across the screen that follows
whatever your camera orbits, with blur above and below it that gets stronger with depth.
Bright points spread into soft bokeh discs, and a colour grade gives the scene the bright,
slightly toy-like colours of a painted model. You can change every part of it while you play.

The panel also has lens controls (field of view, a flat view without perspective, lens
shift, a longer camera distance) and access to the game's own distance blur and colour
grading. There is a frame limit for a stop-motion feel, and the mod can draw rain, snow and
hail that blur with the rest of the picture and follow the game's weather. Looks you like
can be saved as profiles, each on its own hotkey.

Three optional extras are off until you switch them on: [static cameras](static-cameras.md)
that you place around the farm, [preview windows](preview-windows.md) that show those
cameras live, and a [camera timeline](timeline.md) that cuts between them by itself. The
timeline was built for filming timelapses of your workers in the field.

## Quick start

1. Install the mod. Copy `FS25_TiltShift.zip` into your mods folder
   (`Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`), or download it from ModHub in the game,
   then activate it for your savegame.
2. Switch to a third-person view. The effect only works on orbit cameras: on foot in third
   person, or a vehicle's outside camera.
3. Press **Right Ctrl + J** to turn the effects on. They are off each time you load a game,
   but your settings are kept.
4. Press **Right Ctrl + K** to open the panel. [The panel](panel.md) explains how to get
   around it, [Looks and profiles](looks-and-profiles.md) covers the ready-made looks and
   saving your own, and [Effects](effects.md) lists what each setting does.

![A farmyard from a raised static camera, with the miniature tilt-shift look](assets/hero.jpg)

!!! tip "Where the effect applies"
    Cab views, first person and fixed vehicle cameras are left alone. Free and cinematic
    cameras from other mods get the effect too, because it follows whichever camera is
    active. The shop, the workshop, build mode and the sleep screen always show the plain
    game.

## Extras

Static cameras, preview windows and the camera timeline stay off until you turn them on in
the EXTRAS rows of the panel's TILT SHIFT section. Each one depends on the one before it,
so its switch only appears once that one is on:

1. **Static Cameras**
2. **Preview Windows**, once static cameras are on
3. **Camera Timeline**, once preview windows are on

## Multiplayer

The mod works in multiplayer. It only changes your own screen, and each player has their
own cameras and timeline.
