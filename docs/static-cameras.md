# Static cameras

A static camera is a tripod you leave in the world. Plant a few around a field, then switch
between them while you keep driving, walking and working: the game carries on as normal
while the camera films.

!!! info "Switch them on first"
    Open the panel (**Right Ctrl + K**) and set **Static Cameras** in the EXTRAS rows to ON.
    The STATIC CAMERAS section then appears in the panel.

## Adding and switching

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Right Ctrl + N** | Plant a camera exactly where your view is now |
| **Right Ctrl + C** | Switch to your last static camera, or back to the live view |
| **Right Ctrl + V** | Go to the next static camera |

New cameras are called Camera 1, Camera 2 and so on. A message at the top of the screen
tells you which camera you are looking through.

While a static camera is on screen:

- On foot, the game switches you to third person so that you stay visible in the shot.
- The game's own camera key (**C**) is taken over until you go back to the live view, so it
  cannot pull you out of the shot.
- Getting into or out of a vehicle keeps the static camera on screen.
- Menus, the sleep screen and cinematic camera mods still take over as usual.

## The STATIC CAMERAS section

View
:   What is on screen: **Live** or one of your cameras. Left and right switch between them.

Add Camera Here
:   The same as **Right Ctrl + N**.

Edit Camera
:   Picks the camera the rows below it change. The heading above it names that camera.
    Press **Enter** to look through it.

Look
:   The look this camera uses: **Own**, its own tilt-shift settings, or one of your saved
    profiles. A new camera starts with a copy of the look you had when you planted it. With
    **Own**, any change you make while looking through the camera is kept with it. See
    [Looks and profiles](looks-and-profiles.md#static-cameras-and-profiles) for how a camera
    that uses a profile behaves.

Move to My View
:   Moves the camera to where your own view is now, keeping its look.

Preview Window
:   Shows or hides this camera's [preview window](preview-windows.md).

Fly Into Place
:   Fly the camera into place yourself. See below.

POSITION AND AIM
:   Fine-tune the camera by the numbers. **Position X**, **Height** and **Position Z** move it
    in steps of 0.25 m (2 m with Page Up and Page Down). **Turn**, **Tilt** and **Roll** turn
    it in steps of 1 degree (10 degrees). **Field of View** sets its field of view from 5 to
    150 degrees.

Delete Camera
:   Deletes the camera after you confirm it in the game's own Yes/No dialog.

## Flying a camera into place

Choose **Fly Into Place** in the panel, or the fly button on the camera's preview window.
The panel closes and you look through the camera:

- **W A S D** (your own movement keys) move it,
- the **mouse** turns it,
- hold **Shift** to move fast,
- **Enter** saves the new position,
- **Esc** cancels and puts the camera back where it was.

The panel opens again afterwards, still looking through the camera.

## On the map

Every static camera has a marker on the pause-screen map and on the minimap. Select a
marker on the big map and use **Switch to Camera** to look through it, or
**Delete Camera** to delete it (after the same Yes/No question).

## Saving

Cameras belong to your savegame and are written when you save the game. In multiplayer
each player has their own cameras.

Turning **Static Cameras** off in the panel hides everything to do with them and brings
you back to the live view. Your cameras are kept for when you switch it on again.
